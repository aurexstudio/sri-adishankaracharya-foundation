चार मठ

The Four Mathas

Traditionally associated with Adi Shankaracharya, the four Amnaya Mathas are revered centres of learning and spiritual guidance in the four directions of India. Each carries forward the study of Vedanta and the enduring wisdom of Sanatana Dharma.

The Veda, mahāvākya and first-Acharya associations above follow the traditional listing published by Sri Sringeri Sharada Peetham. Traditions and spellings may vary across sources.

शिष्य परम्परा

His Four Principal Disciples

Traditional accounts highlight four disciples for distinctive strengths: Padmapada for devotion, Totaka for exemplary service, Hastamalaka for self-realisation, and Sureshwara for deep learning. They are traditionally associated with the four Mathas shown above.
